## `01 — about`

I make things that probably didn't need to exist.

Sometimes they're poems.  
Sometimes photographs.  
Sometimes drawings.  
Sometimes a Linux configuration that takes three hours to fix and five seconds to break.

I have a particular weakness for **old technology, strange aesthetics, imperfect photographs, surrealism, music and things that feel slightly out of place.**

I prefer making something interesting over making something normal.

## `08 — philosophy`

> **I don't want everything to make sense.**

Some things are better left unresolved.

A photograph can be technically imperfect and still be beautiful.  
A poem can be meaningless and still mean something.  
A machine can be obsolete and still be worth keeping.

The interesting parts are usually somewhere between the categories.
